About

Dr. Gunikhan Sonowal is a cybersecurity researcher and academic specializing in digital forensics, machine learning for threat detection, anti-phishing technologies, and accessible security design. He is currently a Postdoctoral Fellow in Digital Forensics at the National Forensic Sciences University, Gandhinagar, under the ISEA Phase-III programme funded by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India.

He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from Pondicherry University and a Master's degree from the University of Hyderabad. With over six years of teaching experience as an Assistant Professor at Assam down town University, KLEF, and Sanskriti School of Engineering, JNTUA, he has authored 15+ research papers, 4 books, and holds 1 patent. His current research focuses on digital forensics, forensic data deduplication, and AI-driven threat detection.
